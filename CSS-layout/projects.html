My Projects

Here are some of the projects I have worked on while exploring computer science, design, technology, and their impact on people.

Georgia Period Tax Education Platform

A digital education platform created to help Georgia residents understand the state's tax on menstrual products and its impact on women and low-income communities.

The project focuses on creating clear, stigma-free educational materials and infographics.

A prototype campus navigation application designed to help students find accessible routes around campus.

PathFinder Connect

A Python-based prototype created for my Ethics of Computing coursework. The project explored how AI could help users navigate resources while considering the needs of underserved communities.

I conducted user research and developed personas to identify unmet needs among low-income individuals, immigrants, and first-generation students. I also examined algorithmic bias, privacy, and accessibility risks and developed strategies to address them.
