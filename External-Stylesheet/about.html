About Me

Hello!

My name is Mariam, and I am a Computer Science student at Georgia State University. My path through technology has not exactly been straightforward. I have considered fields ranging from biomedical engineering and cybersecurity to software engineering, industrial design, and UX/UI. Even though those fields are very different, I think what connects them is my desire to create something that can help people.

I have always wanted to make a positive difference in the lives of the people around me and, hopefully, in the lives of people I may never meet. Sometimes that has made choosing one career difficult because there are so many different ways I could imagine doing that. I could protect people through cybersecurity, create useful products through design, build technology through software, or design experiences that make technology easier for people to use. I am still figuring out which direction is right for me, and I think that is okay.

Why I Created This Site

I created this site because I wanted to make something of my own while continuing to explore the creative side of technology. I enjoy the process of turning an idea into something people can actually see and interact with. Choosing the layout, colors, fonts, and overall look of a page gives me a different kind of satisfaction than simply writing code. It feels like a place where my technical and creative interests can meet.

More than anything, I want the things I create to have a purpose. Whether that eventually means designing a product, building software, protecting people online, working with technology in healthcare, or creating something completely different, I want to look back and know that I used what I learned to make a positive difference. I am still figuring out exactly what that looks like, but for now, I am giving myself room to learn, create, and see where it takes me.
